## Brand & Style

This design system expresses the high-value architectural prestige, generational wealth, and institutional authority demanded by premier property investment in DHA Phase 9 Prism, Lahore. The visual tone is commanding yet welcoming, merging high-end luxury real estate marketing with contemporary architectural clarity. 

The aesthetic is grounded in a hybrid of **Modern Minimalist Editorial** and **Tactile Architectural Framing**:
- Clean, open spaces reflecting expansive plots and grand master-planned infrastructure.
- High-contrast typography capturing authoritative market analysis and transparent transactional trust.
- Subtle warm-gold accents balanced against deep maritime navy foundations and natural travertine/marble backgrounds.
- Tactile refinements such as precision borders, floating pill navigation bars, and glassmorphic micro-details that evoke premier building finishes.

## Colors

The palette balances monumental prestige with high-conversion clarity.

- **Prestige Deep Navy (`#0B1329`)**: Serves as the primary anchor for headers, structural brand containers, and high-impact hero modules, establishing unmatched gravitas and security.
- **Royal Sovereign Gold (`#D4AF37`)**: The primary luxury accent. Supported by `#B99A59` (antique gold for borders and muted badges) and `#F59E0B` (radiant gold for dynamic micro-interactions and call-to-action highlights).
- **Architectural Blue (`#1E3A8A`)**: An interactive accent used for secondary actions, interactive map pins, sector outlines, and verified property badges.
- **Slate & Travertine Canvas (`#FFFFFF`, `#F8FAFC`, `#F1F5F9`)**: Clean, marble-inspired surfaces that give the user interface room to breathe and allow photography of DHA developments to stand out.
- **Charcoal Type (`#0F172A`)**: Ensures uncompromising legibility for editorial property details, price matrix tables, and investment dossiers.

## Typography

The pairing of **Manrope** and **Plus Jakarta Sans** establishes an interplay between geometric architectural precision and ergonomic readability.

- **Manrope** lends headlines a modern European architectural cadence with clean geometry and balanced counters, echoing structural crossbeams, pillars, and grand entrance portals.
- **Plus Jakarta Sans** provides a friendly, ultra-legible body texture suitable for complex financial figures, plot dimensions (e.g., 5 Marla, 10 Marla, 1 Kanal, 2 Kanal), and technical documentation.
- **All-caps subheaders (`label-caps`)** are paired with tracking (`0.08em`) to label plot categories, phase statuses, and building milestones with gallery-like restraint.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout adopts an architectural grid system designed for panoramic imagery, plot mapping, and modular listing specifications.

- **Grid System**: A 12-column responsive fluid grid for desktop with a maximum canvas width of `1360px`. It gracefully breaks to an 8-column layout on tablet devices and a 4-column column layout on mobile screens.
- **Rhythm**: Spacing follows a 4px/8px modular base scale. Section margins are generous (`2.5rem` to `5rem` vertical spacing), reflecting the openness and scale of DHA master-planned communities.
- **Responsive Adaptations**:
  - **Desktop (1024px+)**: Dual-pane master-detail layouts for map-based plot exploration, sticky sidebars for investor inquiries, and extended filter bars.
  - **Tablet (768px - 1023px)**: Cards switch to 2-column distribution with horizontal scrolling chip bars for Sector (A through R) navigation.
  - **Mobile (< 768px)**: Edge-to-edge listing carousels, stacked action panels, and a fixed bottom quick-contact pill bar for instant WhatsApp/call scheduling.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses architectural layered planes rather than heavy drop shadows:

- **Surface Layering**: Crisp off-white panels (`#FFFFFF`) rest over light marble slate canvas foundations (`#F8FAFC`), isolated with ultra-fine metallic borders (`1px solid rgba(185, 154, 89, 0.2)`).
- **Floating Pill Layers**: Navigation controls and floating contact badges utilize glassmorphic blur filters (`backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`) with a high-translucency navy or frosted pearl base (`rgba(11, 19, 41, 0.85)` or `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.9)`), edged with a hairline gold sheen.
- **Ambient Lighting**: Floating cards feature subtle, diffused ambient shadows (`0 8px 30px -4px rgba(11, 19, 41, 0.06)`). On hover, elevations subtly shift upward with an ambient warm luminescence (`0 16px 36px -6px rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.12)`).

## Shapes

The design system uses a deliberate shape philosophy: **geometric, crisp structural cards** balanced by **ergonomic pill-shaped navigation and action components**.

- **Level 2 (Rounded)**: Serves as the primary baseline for content containers, property showcase cards, map tooltips, and dialog panels (`0.5rem` / `8px` up to `1rem` / `16px` for outer cards). This maintains the sharp, engineered discipline of architectural blueprints.
- **Full Pill (`9999px`)**: Reserved strictly for interactive tactile elements—floating headers, category filters, call-to-action buttons, status badges, and search inputs—providing human warmth and modern digital sophistication.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Luxury Action**: Solid Deep Navy (`#0B1329`) base with a refined Gold (`#D4AF37`) border, crisp white text, and a rounded pill silhouette (`border-radius: 9999px`). Hover triggers a smooth gold glow and subtle scale lift.
- **Secondary Prestige Action**: Gold Gradient (`linear-gradient(135deg, #D4AF37 0%, #B99A59 100%)`) with Deep Navy text, used strictly for conversion moments (e.g., "Schedule Site Visit", "Book Plot Consultation").
- **Ghost/Outline**: Transparent surface with a 1px border of `#0B1329` or `#D4AF37` and refined letter-spaced typography.

### Pill Navigation Bar
- A floating capsule positioned centered at top or bottom with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`. Encases brand logotype, streamlined sector quick-links, and quick dial options. Padded with `space-xs` and separated by micro gold dividers.

### Property & Construction Cards
- Built with crisp `12px` rounded corners, thin perimeter borders (`#F1F5F9`), and recessed media viewports showcasing plot boundaries or completed residential luxury villas.
- Header contains plot size badges (e.g., "1 Kanal", "Direct Owner") styled as semi-transparent pill overlays.
- Footer features high-clarity numerical pricing in navy, alongside gold verified contractor stamps.

### Chips & Filter Pills
- Compact pill controls used to toggle DHA Phase 9 Prism sectors (e.g., "Sector A", "Sector Q", "Commercial Zone").
- Inactive: `#FFFFFF` with `#E2E8F0` border and `#0F172A` text.
- Active: `#0B1329` background with `#D4AF37` text and an integrated check indicator.

### Input Fields & Search Bars
- Minimalist architectural style: `#FFFFFF` background with a `1px` border in `#CBD5E1`.
- Focused state transitions to a crisp `#D4AF37` outline accompanied by an ambient `0 0 0 3px rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.15)` ring.
- Placeholder text in muted slate (`#94A3B8`). Integrated search icons utilize deep navy and warm gold accents.

### Checkboxes & Radio Selectors
- Checkboxes maintain a neat `4px` radius; radio buttons are circular.
- Unchecked: 1.5px border in `#94A3B8`.
- Checked: Deep navy fill (`#0B1329`) with an interior gold checkmark or inner core, ensuring seamless integration with the royal palette.

### Plot Status Indicators
- Micro pill tags denoting availability:
  - **Available / Open File**: Deep emerald tone on mint (`#065F46` / `#ECFDF5`).
  - **Hot Investment / In Demand**: Royal Gold on champagne (`#92400E` / `#FEF3C7`).
  - **Possession Handed Over**: Deep Prestige Navy on slate (`#0B1329` / `#F1F5F9`).
